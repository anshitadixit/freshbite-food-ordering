About FreshBite 🍕

FreshBite is a modern online food ordering platform designed to make discovering and ordering delicious food simple, fast and convenient.

Our platform allows customers to explore restaurants, browse menus, view detailed food information, add delicious meals to their cart and place orders from the comfort of their home.

FreshBite is developed as a frontend-based food ordering application using HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript. The project uses JavaScript and Local Storage to simulate user accounts, shopping carts and order management without requiring a database.

Our Story 🍴

FreshBite was created with a simple idea: ordering food online should be easy and enjoyable. Our goal is to provide a clean and user-friendly experience where customers can discover food, compare options and manage their orders easily.
